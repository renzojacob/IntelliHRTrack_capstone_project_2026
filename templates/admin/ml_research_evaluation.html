Metric definitions

No single metric by itself establishes a best model.

Recall
How many positive eight-or-more-hour observations the model detected.
Precision
How often positive predictions were actually positive.
F1
Balance between Recall and Precision.
Average Precision / PR-AUC
How well the model ranks positive cases across decision thresholds, especially useful when class frequencies are uneven.
ROC-AUC
Overall ranking and discrimination across thresholds.
Accuracy
Overall fraction of correct predictions.
