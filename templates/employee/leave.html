Good to know

How is the limit counted?

One request counts once, however many days it covers. Pending and approved requests count; drafts, rejected, and cancelled ones do not.

Can I cancel a request?

Yes, while it is still pending.

When does it reset?

Every January 1. Requests are counted by their start date.
