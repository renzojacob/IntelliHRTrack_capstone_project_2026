How your pay is computed

Rates

Daily rate = monthly salary ÷ salary divisor. Hourly = daily ÷ 8. Per minute = hourly ÷ 60.

Attendance

Deductible minutes × per-minute rate. Each minute is deducted once, whether it came from arriving late, a half day, or leaving early.

Job Order

No work, no pay: paid only for days actually worked.

Contract of Service

Fixed pay for the period less unpaid absences. Verified holidays and approved leave are not absences.

Permanent

PERA is non-taxable. Your GSIS, PhilHealth, and Pag-IBIG shares are deducted; employer shares are recorded only.
