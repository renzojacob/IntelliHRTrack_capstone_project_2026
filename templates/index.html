How a working day becomes a payslip

Each step uses the record from the step before it, so HR staff stop retyping logbooks and spreadsheets at the end of every period.

  1. Scan at the terminal

    Employees clock in and out at the Hikvision terminal using face and fingerprint.

  2. Sync to the system

    The office connector sends each scan to IntelliHRTrack and matches it to the right employee.

  3. Credit the hours

    Scans are compared with the assigned shift to compute worked hours, lates, and undertime.

  4. Apply leave and holidays

    Approved leave, holidays, and work suspensions are counted for the payroll period.

  5. Release payroll

    HR reviews the computed payroll, and each employee opens their own payslip online.

Built around how DA MIMAROPA works

IntelliHRTrack was developed for the Department of Agriculture MIMAROPA Region, whose offices serve Occidental Mindoro, Oriental Mindoro, Marinduque, Romblon, and Palawan.

It replaces separate logbooks, spreadsheets, and printed forms with one shared record that HR staff and employees can both rely on.

  • Each office sees its own people

    Branch administrators manage only their office. Employees see only their own records.

  • Payroll comes from attendance

    Pay is computed from recorded scans, schedules, and approved leave rather than manual tallies.

  • Works with the office terminal

    Attendance comes straight from the Hikvision face and fingerprint terminal already on site.
